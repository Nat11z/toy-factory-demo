Our products

We provide one-stop ODM/OEM solutions and have built long-term partnerships with international chain stores, mother-and-baby brands and cross-border e-commerce platforms.

OEM & ODM

Following the philosophy of "integrity as the foundation, innovation for the long run", Sample Toys insists on eco-friendly materials, fine craftsmanship and strict testing, with full-process quality control from mould development and injection moulding to assembly and packaging. Leveraging Chenghai's complete toy supply chain, we provide one-stop ODM/OEM solutions and have built long-term partnerships with international chain stores, mother-and-baby brands and cross-border e-commerce platforms.

 

Looking ahead, the company will keep increasing R&D investment and expand into licensed animation IP, smart toys and durable baby products, striving to become a trusted supplier and partner in the global baby toy market.
